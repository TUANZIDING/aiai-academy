外泌体与细胞外囊泡漫游 · 怎么用Exosome & EV Explorer · How to use

全程两幕 11 站，每站约 6 秒，循环播放。第一幕 · 体内生命周期（纳米尺度）：全景 → EV 家族 → 早期内体 → 多囊体出芽 → 货物装载 → 释放 vs 降解 → 体液漂流 → 被受体细胞摄取。第二幕 · 实验室：差速超速离心 → 其他分离方法 → 鉴定。随时可暂停，或点画面上的发光点、站点列表、左下角定位图直接跳转。Two acts, 11 stops, about 6 seconds each, on a loop. Act I · Life cycle in the body (nanometer scale): overview → the EV family → early endosome → MVB budding → cargo loading → release vs degradation → drifting in body fluids → uptake by recipient cells. Act II · In the lab: differential ultracentrifugation → other separation methods → characterization. Pause at any time, or jump straight to a stop with the glowing dots, the station list or the locator map at bottom left.

全景里注意看：右上方质膜向外鼓出一个琥珀色的小泡并断开（微囊泡，外层翻出磷脂酰丝氨酸）；中央多囊体边缘不断向内出芽；右侧多囊体与质膜融合，一串青色小泡飘向右下的受体细胞。「讲解」抽屉里的生命周期小面板是同一过程的简化动画；进入第二幕后，它换成粒径分布和离心进度（均为示意）。Watch the overview: at upper right the plasma membrane bulges out an amber vesicle that pinches off (a microvesicle, with phosphatidylserine flipped to its outer leaflet); the rim of the central MVB keeps budding inward; on the right an MVB fuses with the plasma membrane and a stream of cyan vesicles drifts toward the recipient cell at lower right. The small life-cycle panel in the Explain drawer is a simplified animation of the same process; in Act II it switches to a size distribution and centrifugation progress (all illustrative).

课堂用法（3 个讨论问题）In class: 3 discussion questions
  1. 一篇论文写"我们用 100,000×g 超速离心分离了外泌体"。按 MISEV2023，这个说法哪里不严谨？应该怎么改写？（提示：大小和密度重叠、内体起源有没有被证明、沉淀里还有什么。）A paper says “we isolated exosomes by ultracentrifugation at 100,000×g”. Under MISEV2023, what is imprecise about this, and how should it be rewritten? (Hint: overlapping size and density, whether endosomal origin was shown, and what else is in the pellet.)
  2. 健康人血浆中 EV 大约 10¹⁰ 个/mL，脂蛋白却多出约六到七个数量级。用 NTA 直接数血浆颗粒，会得到什么？做 EV 液体活检时要怎样设计分离和对照？Healthy plasma holds about 10¹⁰ EVs/mL, yet lipoproteins outnumber them by about six to seven orders of magnitude. What will you get if you count plasma particles directly by NTA? How should separation and controls be designed for an EV liquid biopsy?
  3. 小鼠静脉注射的 EV 几分钟内大部分离开血液，主要进了肝和脾。如果你想用 EV 把药送到肿瘤或脑，可以从哪些方向改？（提示：来源细胞、表面靶向分子、给药途径。）In mice, intravenously injected EVs mostly leave the blood within minutes, ending up mainly in the liver and spleen. If you wanted EVs to deliver a drug to a tumor or the brain, what could you change? (Hint: source cell, surface targeting molecules, route of administration.)

老师小建议：先停在"EV 家族"站，让学员说出三类囊泡各从哪里来；第二幕每站结束时，请学员判断"这一步得到的是纯外泌体吗？"，答案几乎总是"不是，是富集了某类 EV 的混合物"。Teacher’s tip: pause on “The EV family” and have learners say where each of the three vesicle classes comes from; at the end of each Act II stop, ask “is what we have now pure exosomes?” — the answer is almost always “no, a mixture enriched for a certain kind of EV”.

关于数字About the numbers

页面上的尺寸、密度、浓度和离心参数都是文献中的典型值或范围，随细胞类型、样本和方法差别很大。EV 生命周期动画、离心沉降、纯度–得率散点、NTA 粒径分布、活跃度条和事件流都是示意，不是实时测量；时间被大幅压缩（真实的 100,000×g 离心至少 70 分钟）。放大倍数和比例尺按典型尺寸估算。图像由 AI 生成，形状和颜色做了艺术化处理，不代表真实显微照片。文中用「事实 / 联想 / 推测」区分证据强度；动物实验结论不等于人体疗效。Sizes, densities, concentrations and centrifugation settings on this page are typical values or ranges from the literature and vary widely with cell type, sample and method. The EV life-cycle animation, sedimentation, purity–yield scatter, NTA size distribution, activity bars and event log are all illustrative, not real-time measurements, and time is heavily compressed (a real 100,000×g spin takes at least 70 minutes). Magnification and scale bars are estimated from typical sizes. Images are AI-generated with stylized shapes and colors; they are not real micrographs. The text labels evidence strength as “fact / association / speculation”; findings in animals do not equal efficacy in humans.
